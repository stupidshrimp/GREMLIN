About

The Process Reliability team's digital toolkit.

GREMLIN — Graphical Reliability Engineering, Maintenance, Life-Data Analysis INterface — puts the analysis, the trackers, the dashboards and the links this team works from behind one address. It is built by the people who use it, for the work they already do: understanding why a piece of equipment failed, and deciding what to do about it next.

Primary purpose

Investigate root cause events, and plan what follows

GREMLIN is a digital toolkit for the Process Reliability team. It carries analysis and trackers at two altitudes at once — the engineering detail that finds a root cause, and the leadership view that turns a set of those findings into a maintenance strategy — and both are drawn from the same records, so the two never disagree about what happened.

Engineering level

Work orders and PM resets are dispositioned into failure modes and mechanisms, then fitted with life distributions and compared on model confidence. That is what moves a failure from a date on a calendar to a cause with evidence behind it.

Leadership level

The same records roll up into availability, MTBF, MTTR, downtime drivers and PM effectiveness, and the fitted models project reliability out to a mission time. That is what an interval, a spares decision or a strategy change gets argued from.

It also

Keeps the day-to-day in one place

One place for links and data

The dashboards, portals, SharePoint documents and engineering systems this team reaches for are collected on one page instead of scattered across bookmark bars, and the work order and asset data behind every analysis is pulled from the CMMS rather than re-keyed.

A dashboard to leave up

The metrics pages are meant to be displayed, not just opened: maintenance and operations can read availability, downtime and PM status off a screen without anybody being asked to run a report first.

Time given back

Every part of this is the same goal from a different side — cutting out the wasted time and energy of hunting for a file, a link or a number, so more of the day goes to the work those numbers are for.
