Availability explained

How asset availability is calculated

Availability shows the share of scheduled production time that an asset was able to run during a completed calendar month.

Scheduled hours − counted downtime Scheduled hours × 100
  1. Scheduled time. Weekdays in the month are multiplied by the asset group’s daily production hours. Breaks, lunch, and setup time are removed, then any recorded overtime is added. Overtime hours can be added by clicking the top right of an asset group graph.
  2. Count the downtime. Positive downtime from every work order created in that month is included, regardless of the work order’s name, type, or classification. If configured, a share of downtime from a linked asset is also included. The configuration can be edited on the Settings page. Please refrain from editing values unless you are authorized to do so.
  3. Calculate the percentage. Counted downtime is subtracted from scheduled hours, then divided by scheduled hours. For example, 160 scheduled hours and 8 downtime hours produces 95% availability.
Assumptions and flags

Downtime is counted in the month of the work order’s created date in the current dashboard logic. Because of this, a work order’s downtime hours can fall in a different month than the month in which the work was completed.

A work order with positive downtime is flagged in the data view when its completion date is in a different calendar month from its created date, or when its created date plus its downtime duration crosses a month boundary. Work orders with zero or negative downtime are not flagged for crossing a month boundary. A month is flagged when its total counted downtime, including linked downtime, exceeds its adjusted scheduled hours. When adjusted scheduled hours are positive, its availability is shown as 0%; when adjusted scheduled hours are zero, availability is undefined and shown as “—”.

How to read the card

Each bar is one asset’s monthly result. The Average line gives each asset in the group equal weight, while the Goal line shows the configured target. Only completed months are displayed, and the metric is not affected by the asset or date filters above.
