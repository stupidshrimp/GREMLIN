Analysis Population

Choose which dispositioned work orders to look at, triage them by location, component and mechanism, then narrow to one item and fit its life. The same selection drives every view. A Weibull life is always measured on one physical item — a location's component, from one renewal of it to the next — and only then are those lives pooled; the selection decides which items are pooled and which events count as failures, never what a life is measured between.
