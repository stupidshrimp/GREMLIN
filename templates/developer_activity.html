Accounts

Every account, including ones with nothing in the window — an account that is never used is as much a finding as a busy one. “Per active day” separates somebody who signs in daily from somebody who signed in six times in one afternoon. An account older than the recorded history reads “None recorded since …” rather than “Never signed in”: nothing can reconstruct sign-ins from before this page existed, and an account in daily use must not be reported as unused.
